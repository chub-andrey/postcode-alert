# PostcodeAlert

Emergency alerts for the UK, by postcode: floods, severe weather, power cuts and poor air quality.

Add the postcodes you care about (home, parents, work) and get only the alerts that affect them, with clear advice on what to do and who to call.

> **Status:** early development. Nothing is usable yet.

## What it will do

- Flood alerts and warnings from the Environment Agency
- Met Office severe weather warnings
- Unplanned and planned power cuts from network operators
- High air pollution forecasts from DEFRA
- What to do, who to call and which local BBC radio station to tune in to

## Principles

- Alerts are rare, relevant and actionable. Noise is a bug.
- Only the postcodes you added, nothing else.
- Minimal personal data, and you can delete it at any time.
